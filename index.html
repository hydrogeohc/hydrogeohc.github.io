Research

Two tracks, one question

How do we know an AI system will behave when it matters? I approach that from both ends — the mechanics of agent safety and alignment, and the messy, high-stakes domains where models meet the real world.

01

AI Safety & Alignment

Agentic AI safety, LLM alignment, and the evaluation frameworks that make safety claims checkable rather than aspirational.

  • Red-teaming pipelines with adversarial generation and multi-agent simulation — +30% vulnerability detection
  • Interpretability and transparency for high-stakes decisions (clinical, safety-critical geospatial)
  • Fairness-driven tuning and bias mitigation for multi-agent systems
  • Evaluation metrics, dataset governance, and risk scoring with OWASP AI Risk groups
  • Prompt-injection defense, hallucination detection, and content moderation modules (CIRISAI)

02

Geospatial & Climate Intelligence

Multimodal earth-observation models and forecasting systems built for climate resilience, from foundation-model fine-tuning to terabyte-scale pipelines.

  • Geospatial foundation models and Vision Transformer fine-tuning for wildfire and crop-residue burning detection
  • Bayesian inference, graph neural networks, and time-series models for environmental systems
  • Multimodal classification across satellite imagery and ecological survey data for species protection
  • Distributed training and inference at scale (Dask, Kubernetes, CUDA, OpenMPI, GCP/AWS)
  • Cross-agency GeoAI collaboration at national scale
